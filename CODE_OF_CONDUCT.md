# Code of Conduct

We want UJM to be a friendly place to learn, test and build. This short code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux or to controllers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticize the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you do not know.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal data, and redact your own logs.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or sexualized language or attention.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (**Security → Report a vulnerability**) or contact the repository owner [@joaovitor-dv](https://github.com/joaovitor-dv) through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.
