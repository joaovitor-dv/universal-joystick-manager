Volunteer testers

People who send a reproducible report for a controller model, connection or mode are built into these credits, under the name or handle they choose, and only if they say so.

    Own a controller we cannot test? Send a test report and fill in the Credit name field.

    Software UJM builds on

    • The Linux kernel input and HID drivers (hid_nintendo, hid_sony, hid_playstation, xpad, …) and their maintainers.
    • joycond by Daniel Ogorchock, which combines Joy-Cons and also manages Pro Controllers.
    • BlueZ, systemd and udev.

    Theme

    Pick your own colors. Dark themes only: this window has no light side.

    Your choice is applied instantly and saved in this browser only.
    Semantic colors (A/B/X/Y, LEDs) are never changed.

    Pair a new controller

    1. Put the controller in pairing mode (Joy-Con and Pro Controller: hold the small sync button until the player LEDs sweep; for other controllers see their manual).
    2. Press Scan and pick it from the list.
    3. UJM pairs, trusts and connects it, and it takes the next free player slot.
    Nothing yet. This is a simulation: the prototype does not talk to BlueZ.

    With joycond running, press L + R on a Pro Controller to finish its logical pairing.
